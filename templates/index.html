How Our School System Works

From losing an item to safe verification and handover in five simple steps.

1

LOST

A student misplaces a personal belonging on school campus.

2

REPORTED

Either the owner or the finder submits details to the portal.

3

MATCHED

Our intelligent 100-point algorithm identifies possible item matches.

4

CLAIMED

The rightful student submits verification details for admin review.

5

RETURNED

After staff verification, the belonging is safely handed back.
